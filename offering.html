Core Offering & Products

The company offers a range of modular solutions designed to maximize retail and warehouse efficiency. Manufacturers typically provide a diverse portfolio of racking systems tailored to specific load capacities and accessibility needs:

  • Retail Display Systems: Custom-designed, adjustable racks and display solutions suitable for hypermarkets, supermarkets, and departmental stores.
  • Showroom Fixtures: Specialized setups for electronic and garment showrooms.
  • Heavy-Duty Pallet Racks: Essential for storing palletized goods with high load-bearing requirements.
  • Industrial Coating: Professional services including powder coating (automated and decorative) for aluminum and construction equipment, as well as electrostatic coating.

Manufacturing Process & Quality Standards

  • Materials: Primarily utilize high-grade, cold-rolled, or hot-rolled steel (e.g., TATA or JSW) for maximum strength.
  • Advanced Fabrication: Use of CNC machinery, Laser Cutting machinery and automated production lines to achieve zero-error tolerance and consistent quality.
  • Finishing: Components often undergo 7-tank powder coating or anti-corrosive treatments to resist rust and wear in harsh industrial environments.
  • Certifications: Reputable firms typically maintain ISO 9001 certifications and adhere to strict safety standards.

Business Profile and Operations

  • Ethical Practices: The company is noted for adopting ethical business practices and aggressive marketing strategies.
  • Project Execution: Recognized for on-time execution of store display projects with ergonomic and functional designs.
  • Manufacturing Capabilities: Facilities include advanced machinery such as robotic and ARC welding.

Destino Steels Industries Private Limited is a specialized manufacturer based in Odisha, India, that provides adjustable display and storage solutions tailored for various retail environments. The company focuses on delivering high-quality, design-driven systems for large-scale retail projects.
